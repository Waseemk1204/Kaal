- **Embed options:** viewport **1280 × 720**, **Fullscreen button** on, **Mobile friendly** off,

- **Genre:** Horror. **Tags:** horror, atmospheric, first-person, psychological-horror, short, 3D, India.
- **Cover image:** 630 × 500. A good one is the moonlit dining room with Kaal at the edge of the light.

## Page text (paste this)

> **KAAL**
>
> *As long as the lamp burns, Kaal waits.*
>
> A Sunday dinner in 1987. Dadi tells you what Kaal looks like: so tall it bows at
> every door, no face, because faces are the first thing it takes. Then the power
> goes.
>
> Strike a match and the house comes back as it was, but only as far as the light
> reaches, and only while it burns. In the dark it is the same house forty years
> later: rotted, rooted through, condemned, empty. Bring your family back to the
> table before your matches run out. Something very tall is walking toward you,
> and it has never once had to hurry.
>
> A first-person horror game about how everything is temporary: the light, the
> past, the people in it, and you. 15–25 minutes. Two endings.
>
> **Controls:** WASD walk · mouse look · F or right-click strike a match · E use (hold for longer tasks) ·
> Q set down a candle · G drop a burning match · Tab count your matches · Shift run (blows out a match) · Esc pause
>
> **Content warning:** gore, death, loss and grief, and flickering lights.
> *Settings → Reduce flashing lights* softens the flicker. Best with headphones, in the dark.
> Runs in the browser on a Windows PC with keyboard and mouse. If it's slow, try *Settings → Graphics → Low*.
>
> **Theme, Everything is Temporary:** light is the only way back to 1987, and every flame
> burns down. Things you bring back from the past age in your hand in the dark.
> The monster is time itself.
>
